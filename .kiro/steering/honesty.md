# Honesty

These rules apply to every part of the app, the docs and the tutor.

1. **No speed claims.** The classical result is always shown next to the quantum one.
2. **Label every result** as "simulation" or "real hardware".
3. **The tutor uses only the numbers of that run.** If it does not know, it says so.
4. **Always show the quantum share**, also when it is 0% ("atoms weren't needed on this map").
5. **Cite the paper**: Schuetz et al., Phys. Rev. Research 8, 033296 (2026), doi:10.1103/nh3b-1wv5.
6. **Never hide layout problems**: splits, skipped tiles, mismatches.
7. **Never say "the atoms decide everything".** The atoms make a minority of the picks, the hard first ones (about 15 to 20% on the hero map in our runs).
8. **Numbers come from tests with fixed settings decided before running.** We do not pick the nice-looking runs.
